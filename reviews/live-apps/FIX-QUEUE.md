Linux X11 with Chromium, the remaining Craft work, and ACAD are the release priorities. All 35 updated game sources are on GitHub and passed their native suites and local production-bundle launch and close. All 35 game updates are live. The seven Learning & Strategy games passed real play and Files save, reopen, and continued play in guest and signed-in sessions. [Learning game release and pins](learning-games-release.md) Keep each finding open until a live test exercises the reported behavior. [Hosted game checks and screenshots](game-update-checks.md) · [Independent game harness](https://github.com/CC-David-CC/webos-game-harness)

## Work order

1. **Anon desktop regression and opening view.** Restore browser-local Files and WordCraft. Audit the other enabled document apps in fresh Anon sessions, starting with PCBCraft. Open Weather Maps over the US on fresh anonymous entry. Check the account transitions below before releasing.
2. **Linux X11 and Chromium.** Reproduce the exact 32-bit browser build, exercise local HTML and input, and test scoped networking with a temporary permitted account. Keep ordinary and anonymous guests on loopback.
3. **Craft apps.** Follow the open rows below. Verify each affected native edit, account save, reopen from Files, recovery, and compact interaction on the deployed version. Keep verified releases distinct from pending hardware checks.
4. **ACAD Modeler.** Qualify the source-built engine under the site's existing browser security policy. Create, save and reopen the same editable model through Files.
5. **Remaining app reviews and games.** Continue the individual findings below. All 35 updated games are now live, including the seven verified Learning & Strategy games.
