# Stitch Converter

On ksu.codes, a disposable account changed the heart artwork from red to purple and set its design size to 64 mm on a phone-sized screen. It saved the editable SVG to Files, generated a stitch preview, exported a PES file and reopened the exported file. New tablet, 1080p and 4K browser sessions reopened the phone-created SVG from the account. All three recovered the color and size, exported a fresh PES and decoded it.

| Screen | Result |
|---|---|
| [Phone, 390 × 844](phone.png) | Edited the source, saved artwork, exported and reopened the PES. In the captured window, the controls push the design below the visible area. |
| [Tablet, 820 × 1180](tablet.png) | Reopened the source and converted it. In the captured window only the top of the reopened design is visible. |
| [Desktop, 1920 × 1080](desktop.png) | Reopened both formats. The full purple design and status fit in view. |
| [4K, 3840 × 2160](4k.png) | Reopened both formats. The design remained relatively small in the maximized window. |

**Dimensional fidelity is unresolved.** Every layout reported command bounds of 58.9 × 57.4 mm in the source preview and 53.8 × 48.4 mm after PES export and reopen. Both measurements include travel moves. The review does not establish whether the sewn outline changed. Compare sewing and travel bounds independently with a reference decoder before relying on the size.

For compact machine-file viewing, put the design ahead of source-edit and export controls. Touch input was emulated. Physical phone and iPad interaction, a real machine sew-out and other export formats remain unverified.
