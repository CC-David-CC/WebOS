Independent local repository ownership does not make a project first-party or community; these are maintained local forks preserving their actual upstream identities.

Primary provenance: [ArtCraft official catalog](https://getartcraft.com/apps) lists the16 included first-party apps plus Craft Anything. [WordCraft upstream README](https://github.com/storytold/wordcraft#the-crafting-apps) explicitly labels SolidCraft as a Tangency Labs community app. The local SolidCraft upstream remote is tangency-labs/solidcraft.

Our selected17 are not identical to the official catalog17: Craft Anything was excluded by the user and SolidCraft was included. MailCraft is also excluded. This records current selection; it does not add apps or claim browser readiness.
