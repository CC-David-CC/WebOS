Primary provenance: [ArtCraft official catalog](https://getartcraft.com/apps) lists the16 included first-party apps plus Craft Anything. [WordCraft upstream README](https://github.com/storytold/wordcraft#the-crafting-apps) explicitly labels SolidCraft as a Tangency Labs community app. The local SolidCraft upstream remote is tangency-labs/solidcraft.

The WebOS selection includes SolidCraft and SketchCraft. Craft Anything and MailCraft are outside this rollout. Check the [integration matrix](CRAFT-INTEGRATION-MATRIX.md) for browser readiness.
